iPhone energy — 2026-07-19, battery-delta (part 2: the int8-activation energy answer)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Protocol: unplugged, Wi-Fi-driven, 600 s sustained decode per arm, 1%-step battery API
(energySource battery-1pct), 300 s cooldowns. Both captured arms happened to burn exactly
5% in their 600 s window -> identical joules (2,970 J), so the cleanest framing is tokens
per charge. Sustained regime: both arms entered at thermal "fair" and peaked "serious" —
that IS the sustained operating point; regime held equal across arms.

mlx-swift   PTQ 4-bit        0.151       19,640          29.6  (64% of 46.4)
mlx-swift   QAT OptiQ        0.207       14,336          23.3  (67% of 34.8)   <- the +7-GSM8K-pt build costs +37% J/tok on device
llama.cpp   Q4_K_M           BLOCKED — adapter empty-output bug (zero tokens on every task
                             except short-chat; prefill runs, Mac llama-server fine; the
                             zero-decode sustain loop never accumulates, hence the spin)
core-ai     own int4         0.352*      8,448          19.0  (56% of 34.2)  *shallow-rep

ANSWER: on its native iPhone path, wNa8o8/LiteRT wins energy too — 24% more tokens on the
same 5% of battery (0.122 vs 0.151 J/tok) — REVERSING the Mac result (Mac: MLX 0.090 vs
litert-WebGPU 0.154). The co-designed weights+runtime package holds all three of
speed / memory / energy on device; quality remains MLX-OptiQ's (91.0 vs 85.0).
LiteRT also throttles less under sustained load (76% vs 64% of burst rate retained),
consistent with the README's throttling section.

core-ai row, standard (deep) protocol: SIGKILL (jetsam) reproduced down to
--sustain-seconds 30. Cause is the known depth wall (report 注4: working set +0.25 GB
@depth128, +1.06 @256, +3.47 @512 -> kill): EnergyTask decodes to maxTokens=2048 per rep,
far past the ~depth-400 kill line. That failed-run finding stands (fairness rule 4).
The 0.352 number comes from the SHALLOW-REP variant (--max-tokens 192/rep — the CLI knob
already existed for exactly this) which keeps depth < the wall. Shallow attention is
CHEAPER per token, so 0.352 is a favorable-bias lower bound — and core-ai still uses ~2.9x
litert's deep-protocol energy per token (8,448 vs 24,336 tokens on the same 5% of battery).
The planned litert-shallow calibration bridge was skipped (battery hit 15%); it can only
push core-ai's deep-equivalent HIGHER, so the ranking is settled without it. Optional
refinement: run litert-lm energy --max-tokens 192 at >40% battery and compute
core-ai_deep-equiv = 0.352 x (0.122 / litert_shallow).
